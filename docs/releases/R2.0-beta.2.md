## Changes

Gene cluster families recomputed. The BGCs are those of R2.0-beta.1, unchanged; what changed is how they are
grouped, plus the database tables listed below.

- The families were recomputed with the same method (BiG-SLiCE 2.0 at 0.4, seed and fragmented tiers) on this
  release's BGCs, including the 8,490 GTDB BGCs that R2.0-beta.1 added without a family: 762,124 families,
  614,215 of them listed (370,650 seed, 243,565 fragmented). 14,123,632 BGCs (88.4%) are in a family;
  2,402,552 are in more than one. 1,335 listed families contain a MIBiG reference.
- Family names now carry the release their clustering first shipped in: `GCF-v2b2-000042`, `GCF-v2b2-F-400042`.
  Families are numbered afresh in each clustering, so `GCF-v2b2-000042` is unrelated to R2.0-beta.1's
  `GCF-000042`. The previous families are listed in `db/gcf_legacy`, and `db/gcf_successor` says which new
  families each one's members are in (see FAMILY NAMES AND THE PREVIOUS CLUSTERING in `db/README.txt`). On the
  site, a link to a previous family shows the same.
- `db/gcf_hierarchy` (family super-groups) is gone: the new clustering has no such step.
- `clustering-v3/`: the two new BiG-SLiCE runs. They place new BGCs into these families like the previous runs
  did, but their per-gene tables are empty, so BiG-SLiCE's report viewer and re-running at another threshold no
  longer work from them (`clustering-v3/README.txt`).
- Per-study figures follow the new families: % clustered and exclusive GCFs on /studies, `db/study_gcf`,
  `db/gcf_study_count`, `db/study_bgc_summary`.
- New table `db/genomes.tsv.gz` (also in the TSV and Parquet bundles): one row per genome (isolate, MAG,
  SAG) with CheckM2 completeness and contamination, the MIMAG label, the source of each genome's values
  (`checkm_source`: our CheckM2 1.1.0 run, or GTDB R232's published CheckM2), lineage and BGC count.
  `db/README.txt` documents it under GENOME QUALITY.
