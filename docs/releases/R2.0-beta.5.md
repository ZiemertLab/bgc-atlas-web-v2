## Changes

124,176 BGCs added and the gene cluster families recomputed on all 16,109,198. No BGC was removed.

- New BGCs: 66,549 from 173 metalog samples (SPIRE assemblies), and two long-read data sources: Great Barrier
  Reef seawater (`custom_great_barrier_reef`, 38,505 BGCs, PRJEB82623) and the Helgoland 2020 spring bloom
  (`custom_helgoland_2020`, 19,122 BGCs, PRJEB52999). They are in every sequence file, the sequence-search index
  and `bgc_uuid_source`.
- Families recomputed with the same method (BiG-SLiCE 2.0 at 0.4, seed and fragmented tiers): 767,255 families,
  618,695 listed (373,384 seed, 245,311 fragmented). 14,232,505 BGCs (88.4%) are in a family; 2,429,516 are in
  more than one. 1,336 listed families contain a MIBiG reference.
- Family names carry the tag `v2b5`: `GCF-v2b5-000042`, `GCF-v2b5-F-400042`. `db/gcf_legacy` adds the v2b4
  families (`clustering = 'v2b4-2026-10-12'`); `db/gcf_successor` links them to the v2b5 families.
- `clustering-v3/`: the two BiG-SLiCE runs of this clustering.
