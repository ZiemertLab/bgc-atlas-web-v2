## Changes

The 20,189 MGnify assemblies analysed in 2026 were re-analysed. Their genes had been called with prodigal in
single-genome mode, which is meant for one genome, not a metagenome. Contigs of at least 20 kb were re-analysed
with prodigal in metagenome mode (same metaSMASH 1.0.3, antiSMASH 8.0.4 core); regions on shorter contigs,
already gene-called in metagenome mode, were kept from the original run. 6,307 assemblies gained or lost a
region; in the rest, regions on long contigs may have new boundaries or genes.

- 15,985,022 BGCs (+7,638): 10,610 regions are new and 2,972 are gone (`bgc_ids_added.txt.gz`,
  `bgc_ids_removed.txt.gz`). Removed ids are not redirected.
- A BGC id is derived from the region's name, so a region that kept its name kept its id: 1,760,608 did.
  1,384,019 of them are unchanged; 376,589 have new coordinates or a different set of proteins.
- The 387,199 new and changed BGCs are not in a gene cluster family until the families are next recomputed:
  their earlier placement described different genes. 13,744,110 BGCs (86.0%) are in a family, down from
  88.4%; 2,313,679 are in more than one. 3,307 listed families lost all their members; they stay in
  `db/gcfs` with `n_bgc` 0 and are hidden on the site.
- Taxonomy of the new and changed BGCs recomputed as before (mmseqs2 on the region's proteins against GTDB
  R232): 387,003 classified; the 196 that could not be assigned a domain have none. Of the changed BGCs,
  99.8% kept the same lineage.
- Changed files: `all_regions_gbk.tar.gz` with its index and `extract_gbk.py`, `regions_nuc.fasta`,
  `regions_prot.faa` and the sequence-search index built from it, `bgc_uuid_source` (and its README), and
  `db/`. Per-study figures follow (`db/study_gcf`, `db/gcf_study_count`, `db/study_bgc_summary`).
- `clustering-v3/` is unchanged: it describes the R2.0-beta.2 families.

## Known issues

- 387,199 MGnify BGCs have no family (`db/bgc_gcf`) and no row in the clustering's per-BGC table
  (`db/bgc_features`) until the families are recomputed.
