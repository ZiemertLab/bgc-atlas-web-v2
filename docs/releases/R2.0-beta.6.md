## Changes

Gene cluster families corrected and recomputed on R2.0-beta.5's 16,109,198 BGCs, which are unchanged.

- 800,091 families, 629,872 of them listed (373,360 seed, 256,512 fragmented). 14,398,961 BGCs (89.4%) are in a
  family (88.4% in R2.0-beta.5); 2,366,096 are in more than one. 1,328 listed families contain a MIBiG
  reference. The seed run is the same as R2.0-beta.5's, so the seed families are too, with changed members; the
  fragmented families are all new.
- Family names carry the tag `v2b6`: `GCF-v2b6-000042`, `GCF-v2b6-F-400042`. The R2.0-beta.5 families
  (`GCF-v2b5-...`) are replaced without being kept as an earlier clustering: they are not in `db/gcf_legacy`, and
  their names no longer resolve on the site. `db/gcf_successor` links the v2b4 families
  (`clustering = 'v2b4-2026-10-12'`) to the v2b6 families directly.
- Per-family tables follow the new families: `db/gcfs`, `db/bgc_gcf`, `db/bgc_features`, `db/gcf_mibig_anchor`,
  `db/gcf_taxonomy_distribution_mv`. Per-study figures too: % clustered and exclusive GCFs on /studies,
  `db/study_gcf`, `db/gcf_study_count`, `db/study_bgc_summary`.
- `clustering-v3/`: the two BiG-SLiCE runs of this clustering.
- Unchanged: the BGCs and their files (`all_regions_gbk.tar.gz`, `regions_nuc.fasta`, `regions_prot.faa`, the
  sequence-search index, `bgc_uuid_source`).
