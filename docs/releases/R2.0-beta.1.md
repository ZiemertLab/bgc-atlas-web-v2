## Changes

The first versioned release. It is the corpus as of 2026-10-09:

- 46,098 genome-derived assemblies removed (166,457 BGCs): duplicate copies of the same genome, and genomes
  below MIMAG medium quality. Their BGC ids now return 404.
- 1,204 GTDB genomes whose antiSMASH analysis had failed were re-analysed: 8,490 BGCs added.
- Genome quality is CheckM2 for every genome (750,899 of 750,901 genome-collection samples), with its
  provenance per genome: `samples.tsv.gz` has `checkm_completeness`, `checkm_contamination`,
  `genome_quality` (MIMAG high or medium), `checkm_tool` and `checkm_source`, which says whether the values
  are from our CheckM2 1.1.0 run (500,195 genomes) or GTDB R232's published CheckM2 (250,704).
- `regions_nuc.fasta`, `regions_prot.faa` and `all_regions_gbk.tar.gz` now hold every BGC in the release.
  Before, 86 regions were missing from all three and 6,416 more from both FASTAs.

## Known issues

- Gene cluster families have not yet been recomputed after the removals above. Until the next release, some
  GCF member counts and representatives in `db/` describe the corpus before the removals, and the 8,490
  added BGCs belong to no GCF.
