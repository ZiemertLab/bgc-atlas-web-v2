## Changes

Gene cluster families recomputed on R2.0-beta.3's BGCs, which are unchanged. This gives a family again to the 387,199
MGnify BGCs that R2.0-beta.3 re-analysed and left without one.

- The families were recomputed with the same method (BiG-SLiCE 2.0 at 0.4, seed and fragmented tiers): 762,045
  families, 614,268 of them listed (370,690 seed, 243,578 fragmented). 14,128,585 BGCs (88.4%) are in a family;
  2,381,240 are in more than one. 1,346 listed families contain a MIBiG reference.
- Family names carry the new tag: `GCF-v2b4-000042`, `GCF-v2b4-F-400042`. Families are numbered afresh in each
  clustering, so `GCF-v2b4-000042` is unrelated to `GCF-v2b2-000042`. `db/gcf_legacy` now lists the families of
  both previous clusterings (column `clustering`: `v3-2026-08`, `v2b2-2026-10-09`), and `db/gcf_successor` says
  which families of the next clustering each one's members are in: the v2b2 families point at the v2b4 families,
  the August 2026 families still at the v2b2 families. On the site, a link to a previous family shows the same,
  one clustering at a time.
- Per-family tables follow the new families: `db/gcfs`, `db/bgc_gcf`, `db/bgc_features`, `db/gcf_mibig_anchor`,
  `db/gcf_taxonomy_distribution_mv`. `db/bgc_features` again has a row for every BGC.
- Per-study figures follow the new families: % clustered and exclusive GCFs on /studies, `db/study_gcf`,
  `db/gcf_study_count`, `db/study_bgc_summary`.
- `clustering-v3/`: the two BiG-SLiCE runs of this clustering, replacing R2.0-beta.2's.
- Unchanged: the BGCs and their files (`all_regions_gbk.tar.gz`, `regions_nuc.fasta`, `regions_prot.faa`, the
  sequence-search index, `bgc_uuid_source`).
